Turn any small company into an AI-native company, without replacing a single tool.

How it works

1. Connect. Socle plugs into the tools the team already uses (ERP, accounting, CRM, SharePoint, spreadsheets, email), read-only first. Nothing to migrate.

2. Build the company brain automatically. Claude agents read the connected data, extract clients, jobs, projects and costs, recognise the same entity across systems, and propose a company model that a person validates.

3. Work and act. Employees work in Socle Workspace with agents that know the business. When an agent needs to change something, it goes through a governed action.

Most AI platforms start from agents, built one use case at a time. Socle starts from the company brain, so every new agent inherits the same understanding of the business.

A governed action, step by step

  1. Read
  2. Dry-run
  3. Human approval
  4. Logged
  5. Written back through an adapter

Nothing is replaced: employees keep their tools, the company owns its model and data, and it can change model provider without rebuilding.
